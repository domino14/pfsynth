## The piece (Boosey & Hawkes work page, composer's note)

https://www.boosey.com/cr/music/Steve-Reich-Electric-Counterpoint/7542 (read 2026-10-01)

- 1987, ~15′ total, three movements fast–slow–fast played without pause. Commissioned by
  BAM Next Wave for Pat Metheny. Premiere 1987-11-05, BAM, Brooklyn.
- Scoring: "Gtr soloist and gtr ensemble, ensemble consists of 12 gtr and 2 elec. bass gtr
  (ensemble may be live or on tape). CD accompaniment available on rental." The soloist
  pre-records up to 10 guitars + 2 bass parts and plays the 11th guitar part live.
- III. Fast: back to the movement-I tempo and key. A new triple-meter pattern is built into a
  4-guitar canon. Two bass guitars then enter suddenly. The soloist adds strummed chords, built
  into a 3-guitar canon, then melodic resultant patterns. The basses then alternate
  **E minor ↔ C minor** and **3/2 ↔ 12/8** (3×4 vs 4×3 eighths), faster and faster. They fade
  out and the piece resolves in 12/8, E minor.
  (Boosey says the programme note may be reproduced in concert programmes with credit.)
- Boosey also lists *Sean Shibe* (Delphian DCD34213) as a sound sample for III. Fast.
